Item 5.  Other Events.

         Attached as an exhibit is a press release issued by Verizon
         Communications Inc. on November 30, 2000, announcing revised earnings
         targets to reflect the impact of the termination of its merger
         agreement with NorthPoint Communications and steps Verizon is taking to
         compete outside its current wireline footprint. On the same day Verizon
         also held a public conference call during which it indicated that it
         may record a charge in the fourth quarter based on its evaluation of
         the recoverability of its investment of $150 million in NorthPoint
         convertible preferred stock.
