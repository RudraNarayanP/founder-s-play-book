Item 5.  Other Events.

On April 24, 2001, Verizon Communications Inc. issued a press release announcing
its first quarter 2001 financial results, which are attached as an exhibit and
contained in its Investor Relations Bulletin. Also included as an exhibit are
slides which were included in a presentation posted on the Company's website
containing supplemental information about the Company's 2001 earnings per share,
revenue and capital expenditure targets. The Company's website is
www.verizon.com.
