The window opens on **1983-01-01** because that is the year the registrant says it came into legal being, and
it closes on **1984-12-31** because 1984-01-01 is the date the company *started doing something*: it acquired
operating assets and began operations. Ending earlier (at the 1983-10-07 certificate alone) would define the

Subscriber Line Charge authorisation, which the 1994 10-K dates to 1985, and would then collide with the
probe's W-2 (1985-01-01 → 1993-12-31, "independence and scaling as an RHC"). The **first full calendar year of
independent operation** is the natural Stage-1 terminus, and 1986-05-09 (certificate amendment) sits in W-2 by
the same logic.

**Alternative boundaries considered and rejected:** (i) 1994-01-21, the measured first EDGAR existence of this
registrant — rejected: it is an archive floor, not a birth; (ii) 2000-06-30, the d/b/a Verizon launch —
rejected: it belongs to Stage 3's window; (iii) 1877 — rejected outright, because no held byte carries it and
the person it names is not this registrant (`U.1`).

**Boundary confidence: Low**, and honestly so: it is built from recitals written 10-17 years after the events

## §C — Original problem

**STATUS: WRITTEN**

The "original problem" of this registrant is not a market problem; it is a **legal-structural condition**, and
the bytes state it as a fact rather than a ambition: the company exists to hold the exchange-side assets that
AT&T gave up, and it exists inside a cage. The 1994 10-K describes the cage as the reason for the company's
shape: the consent decree "which, together with the Plan of Reorganization ("Plan") approved by the D.C.
District Court, set forth the terms of Divestiture also established certain restrictions on the
post-Divestiture activities of the RHCs, including Bell Atlantic" (`P1S12`).

**KNOWABLE in-period** (per §7 of the method): that local telephone service was regulated, that the company
could not sell long distance in-region, that printed directories and cellular were part of the transferred
asset set. **NOT KNOWABLE in-period from held bytes:** whether management saw the restrictions as a burden or
as a protection; whether anyone planned a growth strategy in 1984. **UNKNOWN** with routes named (periodical
intake FR-5, charter route FR-1).

The 1996 S-4's neutral framing of the same condition is worth one quotation because it is a *third-party-
reviewable* legal document rather than a marketing sentence: "Effective January 1, 1984, American Telephone and
Telegraph Company ("AT&T") divested itself of seven regional holding companies (the "Regional Holding
Companies"), including Bell Atlantic and NYNEX, pursuant to a consent decree approved by the United States
District Court for the District of Columbia" (`P1S05`).
