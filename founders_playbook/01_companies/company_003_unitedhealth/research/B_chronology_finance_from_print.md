## B. EARLIEST RECOVERABLE FINANCIAL SERIES — CONTEMPORANEOUS vs RESTATED

**Convention (adopted in substance from `company_002_walmart/research/A3_audited_series_1962_1980.md`)** — two labels, never interchangeable:

| Label | Meaning | Evidential status |
|---|---|---|
| **CONTEMPORANEOUS** | the value is printed in the report **for that fiscal year** — the document whose own period it describes | registrant self-report, auditor-attested, made at the time; still one lineage, but the earliest possible witness |
| **RESTATED (in `<document>`)** | the value is printed in a **later** document, in a summary or comparative column looking backwards | retrospective; it may differ from what the year itself reported, and where it does, the difference is itself evidence |

A restatement is never presented here as a primary. **A third condition, which this corpus imposes and Walmart's did not:** the FY1994 five-year table announces its *own* double restatement — pooling-of-interests inclusion of two 1994 acquisitions in **all** prior periods, plus retroactive split adjustment (B-026). "RESTATED" here therefore means not merely "printed later" but "printed later **and** re-computed onto a different corporate perimeter", so each row must carry its perimeter.
