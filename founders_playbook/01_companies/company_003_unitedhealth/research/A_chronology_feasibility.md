## Verdict (depth recommendation)

**Recommendation: forensic-core (~6,000 words) for UnitedHealth Group Stage 1 — with a hard escalation gate, and *not* the exemplar.** The reasoning is the dated fact-stock actually recovered, counted rather than felt: for the founding window itself (1974–1990) this probe put **eleven** Tier-1 dated facts on the board, and **every one of them is a governance or legal attribute rather than an operational one** — incorporated January 1977 in Minnesota (UH-08/09/10, five filings, one lineage), `DIRECTOR SINCE 1977` read against "since the Company's inception" (UH-12/13), CEO until February 1988 (UH-12), PHPMN CEO 1977–August 1987 (UH-14), auditor since 1981 (UH-15), five director/officer start dates 1983–1990 (UH-16/17). Add roughly a dozen more Tier-1 dated items for FY1993→2000 (revenue series from 1993, MetLife preferred 1995–1998, the Humana S-4, the 2000 rename, four brand-domain captures 1996–99) and a Tier-2/4 retrospective layer of about seven statements for 1974–88 that reduce to **two lineages** (Star Tribune 2022; a 2006 book + a 2015 Yahoo piece via Wikipedia). That is *less* pre-1990 substance than the Walmart probe carried at its own verdict (22 W-records, "15–20 items"), because Walmart at least had a datable 1962 opening and a 1970 IPO; here the EDGAR floor is **1995-02-02**, the earliest company-authored number is **FY1993**, the first-party web floor is **1996-12-27**, EDGAR full text starts **2001**, and there is **no predecessor CIK at all** (UH-25). Exemplar density (~22,500 words/stage) would have to be manufactured out of the 1998–99 collapse, which the hindsight firewall forbids as founding-window evidence, or out of re-cutting the Burke/Charter-Med lore — the exact padding §1 and §2 prohibit. Forensic-core with strict UNKNOWN discipline is honest and achievable now.

**The escalation gate (this is the finding that matters for the run):** the corpus family that broke the "pre-1994 means no contemporaneous record" inference in the Apple probe — **full-text digitised periodicals on Internet Archive** (Byte 1976–81) — **was NOT checked for UnitedHealth and could not be reported on from this cache.** The only periodical-adjacent probe on disk is a Google Books API call for `chartermed`, which returned `HTTP 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED … quota_limit_value: 0` — an **unanswered** probe, not a proven null (UH-41). No Internet Archive full-text search, no Newspapers.com / Gale / Heritage-Mining / Minnesota Historical Society test, and no title-by-title attempt at *Health Insurance Monthly*, *America's Health Plans* trade materials, *Business Insurance*, *Modern Healthcare*, *MT Adams Health Link Report*, or the Minneapolis/St Paul dailies exists in `sources/`. Consequences for this verdict's calibration, stated plainly: (1) **the verdict's confidence is capped at Medium**, because the one class that could turn Stage 1 from forensic-core into exemplar is precisely the class left untested; (2) nothing in this file may assert that contemporaneous 1974–90 coverage *does not exist* — only that none was found by these endpoints; (3) the run should **fund a bounded periodical probe (D1) before committing the eleven-agent fleet**, because its downside is zero (a documented null) and its upside is the whole stage depth; (4) if D1 lands legible 1977–88 items, escalate Stage 1 toward exemplar and re-run this verdict — the Apple file is the existence proof that this reversal is real, not hypothetical.
