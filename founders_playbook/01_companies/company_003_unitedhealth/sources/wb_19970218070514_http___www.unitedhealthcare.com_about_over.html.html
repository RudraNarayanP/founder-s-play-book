Who is


United HealthCare is a national leader in offering health care coverage and related services, serving purchasers, consumers, managers and providers of health care since 1974. The company serves over 40 million individuals through a broad continuum of health care products and services, including Health Maintenance Organizations (HMOs), Point-of-Service (POS), Preferred Provider Organizations (PPOs) and health insurance products, as well as managed behavioral health services; utilization management; workers' compensation and disability management services; specialized provider networks; third-party administration (TPA) services; employee assistance services; Medicare and managed health care programs for the aged; managed Medicaid services; managed pharmacy; health care evaluation services; information systems; and administrative services.

For more than 20 years, United HealthCare has broadened access, controlled costs, and improved the quality and delivery of health care. We've pioneered numerous management approaches that are now industry standards. We've anticipated our clients' needs with products and services that deliver results and we've made ourselves the industry leader in measuring, reporting, and improving performance.

Sharing intelligence to make a difference

Knowledge has always been power; but in today's health care world, when expertly interpreted and applied, it represents the ability to educate, to heal, to prevent, to learn and to advance.

Our ability to acquire data and make it useful to health care professionals, patients, employers and payers truly distinguishes us from competitors and is at the core of our ongoing competitive and growth strategies. Each and every day, we transform data about treatments, outcomes, costs and plan models into useful information for patients, providers, employers and health care institutions across the nation.
