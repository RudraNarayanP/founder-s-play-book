Review </B></FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:6px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">(a) </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:6px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">UnitedHealth Group Incorporated (the
&#147;Company&#148;) has substantially completed its internal analysis of the October&nbsp;15, 2006 WilmerHale report to the Independent Committee of the Board of Directors (the &#147;Board&#148;) on stock option programs of the Company and is
working expeditiously to complete its final review of accounting adjustments based on the determination of the applicable accounting measurement dates, the impact of variable accounting treatment for certain stock options (which principally relates
to stock options granted in and prior to 2000) and the resulting tax implications. As a result, the Company expects to recognize non-cash charges for stock-based compensation expense which are likely to be material for certain periods covered in the
review. Although the Company is not yet able to determine the final amount of such non-cash charges and additional cash charges resulting from potential tax liabilities, the Company anticipates that it will be significantly greater than the estimate
contained in its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended March&nbsp;31, 2006. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Accordingly, on November&nbsp;7, 2006, management of the Company concluded, and the
Audit Committee of the Board (the &#147;Audit Committee&#148;) approved the conclusion, that due solely to the stock option matter, the Company&#146;s financial statements for the years ended 1994 to 2005, the interim periods contained therein, the
quarter ended March&nbsp;31, 2006 and all earnings and press releases, including for the quarters ended June&nbsp;30, 2006 and September&nbsp;30, 2006 and similar communications issued by the Company for such periods, and the related reports of the
Company&#146;s independent registered public accounting firm should no longer be relied upon. The Company will review its analysis and proposed restatement adjustments with the SEC prior to completing its restatement and is working as quickly as
possible to return to current filing status. The Company&#146;s management and the Audit Committee have discussed the matters disclosed in this Item&nbsp;4.02(a) with Deloitte&nbsp;&amp; Touche LLP, the Company&#146;s independent registered public
accounting firm. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT FACE="Times New Roman" SIZE="2">Additionally, management reevaluated the Management Report on Internal Control over Financial Reporting included in the Company&#146;s
Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December&nbsp;31, 2005 (the &#147;2005 Management Internal Control Report&#148;). The Company has determined that it had a material weakness in internal control over financial reporting relating
to stock option plan administration and accounting for and disclosure of stock option grants as of December&nbsp;31, 2005. The existence of a material weakness as of December&nbsp;31, 2005 would preclude management from concluding that the
Company&#146;s internal control over financial reporting was effective as of December&nbsp;31, 2005. The Company intends to amend its 2005 Management Internal Control Report and expects to receive an adverse opinion on the Company&#146;s internal
control over financial reporting as of December&nbsp;31, 2005 from Deloitte&nbsp;&amp; Touche LLP, the Company&#146;s independent registered public accounting firm. As a result of the material weakness related solely to stock option plan
administration and accounting for and disclosure of stock option grants, the Company also expects to report that its disclosure controls and procedures were not effective as of December&nbsp;31, 2005. </FONT></P>
