## What it would likely answer, if opened through a library
- **A 1990 third-party account of the founding era, two years before the 1992 memoir and
  twelve years before *Made in America* (1992 is the memoir; the museum/press material the
  dossier already has is later still).** It is the earliest *book-length* outsider narrative
  available for the 1945–1962 Ben Franklin years and the 1962 Rogers opening, and its
  chapter titles promise exactly that span ("First five-and-dime days", "An aristocratic
  country town", "A hell of an idea!", "Guinea pig—or death?").
- Candidate detail the corporate reports do not give: the **capital and family terms of the
  first stores** ("$2 and a clean shirt", "$60,000—and no more!", "I need money—quick!"),
  the **1946 partnership with J. L. "Bud" Walton** and the Newport/Versailles sequence that
  the FY1977 and FY1979 reports state in two sentences each, and **Ronald Mayer's route into
  the firm** ("The courtship of Ron Mayer") — the corporate side of that record is already on
  disk and greppable: `WALMART_AR_1973.txt` prints "**Ronald Mayer, Vice President – Treasurer**"
  (the same officers block also prints "Mayer, Vice President. Finance and Treasurer" - comma lost to OCR), while
  `WALMART_AR_1974.txt` lists him as "**Ronald Mayer, Executive Vice President**" — so the
  chapter title is a claim testable against two dated primary mentions.
- **"New chiefs and computers"** is the obvious place to test the museum page's
  IBM System/360 Model 20 claim (Sam Walton attending an IBM class in **1968**, machine
  installed the next year, direct supplier ordering by **1977**) against a non-company
  narrative — that claim currently rests on a 1992 anniversary Barbie insert
  (`walmart_museum_page.html`), i.e. retrospective marketing.
- **"The war over Main Street" / "Labor unions not welcome"** would give a 1990 framing of
  the small-town-impact and union questions that the 1970s reports address only as
  "Security and Loss Prevention" (FY1976) and silence.
- **What it cannot settle:** any figure or date in it is a journalist's 1990 reconstruction,
  so on this project's ladder it ranks **TIER-2 RETROSPECTIVE**, below the report of the
  fiscal year in which the number was first printed. It is *corroboration and colour*, not
  primary financial evidence — and for FY1972–FY1980 that primary layer is now on disk in
  this directory.
