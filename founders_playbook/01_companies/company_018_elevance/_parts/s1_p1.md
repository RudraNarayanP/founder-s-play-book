product → funnel. "Experiment" is read as the first live prepayment pool; "repeatable validation" as the
repetition of an absorbed-plan merger under one operating model; "scalable company" as the 2001 conversion

## G. SUPPLY / HOST SIDE (for an insurer: providers, and the association that hosts the brand)

**G.1 The host is the association, not a supplier.** The Blue Cross / Blue Shield marks, the quality
controls, and the territory all come from BCBSA: the licence preamble names the purposes as "maintaining
uniform quality controls among Plans, facilitating the provision of cost effective health care services to
the public and otherwise benefiting the public", and the plan pays "dues to BCBSA, said dues to represent the
royalties for this License" (S4606 l.20-25, l.182, royalty formula at l.1978-2025). **Independence note for
the merge: the trade name in the market is licensed print, and the 2003 page's own formulation —
"Independent licensees of the Blue Cross and Blue Shield Association" (S4615) — is the registrant saying it
does not own what the sign says.**

**G.2 Providers as the supply side.** "To build our provider networks, we also compete with other health
benefits plans for contracts with hospitals, physicians and other providers. We believe that physicians and
other providers primarily consider member volume, reimbursement rates, timeliness of reimbursement and
administrative service capabilities along with the 'non-hassle' factor" (S4604 l.6666-6672). The claims
mechanics themselves are underwritten by the registrant (benefit expense $6,551.0 m FY2000, S4608 l.1160
region table).

**G.3 The absorbed plans are the other supply line.** Each 1993-2000 event in the recital brings an existing
provider network and territory, and each carries guaranty policy obligations: the conversion provides for
Anthem Insurance "to cease issuing or renewing the Guaranty Policies issued … in connection with its merger
in 1993 with Southeastern Mutual Insurance Company …, its merger in 1995 with Community Mutual Insurance
Company …, and its merger in 1997 with Blue Cross & Blue Shield of Connecticut, Inc." — policies "issued to
protect and preserve the membership interests of the members of the merged mutual companies" (S4605
l.213-222). **A plan absorbed in 1993 still generated contingent obligations in 2001: the cascade was not
free.**

**G.4 1944-1955 supply side: UNKNOWN.** No held byte names a participating Indiana hospital, a contract, or
