<P align="left"><FONT size="2">May&nbsp;20, 2003
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2"><B>Timothy Howard Named Vice Chairman of Fannie Mae&#146;s Board of Directors</B>
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2">WASHINGTON, DC &#150; The Board of Directors of Fannie Mae (FNM/NYSE), the nation&#146;s
largest source of financing for home mortgages, today named Timothy Howard Vice
Chairman, following his election to the Board at the company&#146;s annual meeting
of shareholders. Howard will continue to serve as Fannie Mae&#146;s Chief Financial
Officer.
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2">&#147;Tim&#146;s extraordinary records of service, experience and financial leadership at
Fannie Mae have been key to our disciplined growth, cutting-edge transparency
and long trend of record financial performance. Tim&#146;s new role as Vice
Chairman will further ensure our success in fulfilling the American Dream and
serving the investors who provide the capital to make it possible,&#148; said Frank
Raines, Fannie Mae&#146;s Chairman and Chief Executive Officer.
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2">Howard has responsibility for Fannie Mae&#146;s mortgage portfolio business, which
includes mortgage acquisition, debt issuance, and portfolio strategy. He also
oversees corporate risk management, credit policy, corporate accounting,
strategic planning, and investor relations. Howard joined Fannie Mae as Vice
President and Chief Economist in 1982 and subsequently served as Senior Vice
President &#151; Economics and Planning. He was appointed Executive Vice President
of Economics, Strategic Planning and Financial Analysis in September 1987, and
became Executive Vice President &#151; Asset Management in January 1988. He became
Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer in February 1990.
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2">Prior to joining Fannie Mae, Howard served for five years as Vice President and
Senior Financial Economist at Wells Fargo Bank in San Francisco. In 1975, he
joined the financial advisory group of Chase Econometric Associates, an
economic forecasting and consulting subsidiary of the Chase Manhattan Bank.
During his early career, Howard spent several years working abroad, including
three years in Mexico City organizing an export manufacturing company. He is a
Director of CarrAmerica Realty Corporation. He also is a Trustee, member of
the executive committee, and officer of The Washington Opera, and a member of
the board of the Wharton Financial Institutions Center.
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2">Howard received a master&#146;s degree in economics, and a bachelor&#146;s degree in
economics, magna cum laude, from the University of California, Los Angeles.
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2">In addition to Howard, Fannie Mae&#146;s Office of the Chairman includes Raines, and
Daniel H. Mudd, Vice Chairman and Chief Operating Officer.
</FONT>
<P align="center"><FONT size="2"><B><I># # #</I></B>
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2"><I>Fannie Mae is a New York Stock Exchange company and the largest non-bank
financial services company in the world. It operates pursuant to a federal
charter and is the nation&#146;s largest source of financing for home mortgages.
Fannie Mae is working to shrink the nation&#146;s &#147;homeownership gaps&#148; through a $2
trillion &#147;American Dream Commitment&#148; to increase homeownership rates and serve
18&nbsp;million targeted American families by the end of the decade. Since 1968,
Fannie Mae has provided $4.8 trillion of mortgage financing for more than 52
million families. More information about Fannie Mae can be found on the
Internet at <u>http://www.fanniemae.com.</u></I>
</FONT>
<P align="left"><FONT size="2"><I>Style Usage: Fannie Mae&#146;s Board of Directors has authorized the company to
operate as &#147;Fannie Mae,&#148; and the company&#146;s stock is now listed on the NYSE as
&#147;FNM.&#148; In order to facilitate clarity and avoid confusion, news organizations
are asked to refer to the company exclusively as &#147;Fannie Mae.&#148;</I>
</FONT>
