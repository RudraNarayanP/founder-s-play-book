Item 5.  Other Events

         The Board of Directors of The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc. (the "Company")
has established March 17, 2000 as the date of the Company's 2000 Annual Meeting
of Stockholders. Under the Company's By-laws, any stockholder who intends to
nominate any person or persons for election as a director or directors of the
Company or to bring a proposal before the Company's 2000 Annual Meeting of
Stockholders must provide notice of such proposed nomination or such proposal
to the Secretary of the Company at or before the close of business on December
18, 1999. Such notice must include the information and other materials required
by the Company's By-laws and must otherwise comply with all applicable
provisions of the Company's By-laws and the rules of the Securities and
Exchange Commission.
