## §0. Three corrections to my own brief, before anything else is inherited

**B-1. The 1934/1936/1937 layers are not New York Times microfilm. They are two Hong Kong/South-China English newspapers.**
My brief called them "New York Times Company historical microfilm layers". The bytes say otherwise, three ways:
- The intake sidecars name the route: `sources/corporate_print/NPDP19340704_djvu.txt.meta.json` → `"url": "https://archive.org/download/NPDP19340704/NPDP19340704_djvu.txt"`, and `sources/harvest_mine/_index.json` L16/L42/L68 give the item titles: **"Hong Kong Daily Press 1934-07-04"**, **"Hong Kong Daily Press 1936-07-02"**, **"China Mail 1937-08-23"**.
- `NPCM19370823_djvu.txt` l.1-3: `i OLDEST NEWSPAPER / ~IN THE FAR. EAST. / ESTABLISHED 1845.`
- `NPDP19360702_djvu.txt` l.1 is a marine wind forecast for "South and South-East Winds" (Far-East coasting), and l.15303-15306 of the same file carries the *Hong Kong Daily Press* own agency notice, "The Head Office of the HONG KONG DAILY PRESS, 83, Fleet Street, E.C.4" (OCR: "83, Piatt Sturet, £.0.4").

Consequence, and it is not cosmetic: the corpus's only pre-1955 namings arrive on **Chinese-colonial press pages**, which is why they are almost all *advert* and *market-table* namings of American banks rather than New York reporting on them. A Stage-1 dossier that read these as "US press coverage of the Morgan house" would misstate the provenance class of every one of them.
