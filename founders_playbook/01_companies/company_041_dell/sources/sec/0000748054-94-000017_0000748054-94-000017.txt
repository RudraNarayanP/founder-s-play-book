EXHIBIT A

IDENTITY AND ITEM 3 CLASSIFICATION OF A SUBSIDIARY OF PARENT
HOLDING COMPANIES


     This Schedule 13G is being filed by Twentieth Century Companies, Inc.
("TCC").  Investors Research Corporation ("IRC"), an investment advisor
registered under Section 203 of the Investment Advisors Act of 1940, is a
wholly-owned subsidiary of TCC.  Mr. James E. Stowers, Jr., controls TCC by
virtue of his ownership of approximately 60% of the voting stock of TCC.

     As a result of its status as investment advisor to four investment
companies registered under Section 8 of the Investment Company Act and to
several institutional investors, IRC is deemed to be the beneficial owner of
shares of stock held by such clients.

     TCC, as a result of its control of IRC, and Mr. Stowers, as a result of
his control of TCC, are also deemed to beneficially own all such shares deemed
to be beneficially owned by IRC.  Mr. Stowers, TCC and IRC all disclaim
beneficial ownership of the Shares.
     
     This Schedule 13G is being filed by TCC on behalf of TCC, IRC, TCI and
Mr. Stowers.
