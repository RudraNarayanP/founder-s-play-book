opening"). Where a row rests on one accession family the confidence cell says so and the cap is the one
document's weight. Corroboration requires an **independent origin**: E&Y's signed opinion, an acquired
company's own audited statements filed under Rule 3-05, a counterparty's instrument (the five Buschman
Sales Agreements), a holder-signed Schedule 13G, a pleaded complaint as reported by a defendant, or a
periodical that did not use the filing.

**Hindsight firewall (§2, absolute).** Nothing here treats the company's eventual survival or scale as
evidence that a 1997–1999 decision was rational, that incumbents were fools or asleep, that "selection",
"technology", "the flywheel" or "the platform" was a moat, or that any outcome was foreseeable. The 1999
company **did not know it would survive**, and the record says so in its own voice: the Q3-1999 10-Q's
distribution-risk heading reads "Risk of Distribution Center Expansion" and warns that its new plants
"**may fail to operate properly** to ensure that customer demand is met, particularly during the fourth
quarter of 1999", that it had "**no previous experience with automated distribution centers**", and that
it might be "unable to adequately staff our distribution and customer service centers during these peak
periods". Those are not prudential boilerplate to be read past: they are the contemporaneous state of
mind of the operating layer this volume's recommended boundary is built on, and they are cited as such.
Post-boundary values appear **only as consequences and only under `(PB)`**.

**Record-selection null (§2), per section.** Stated in full at §S in a later volume and previewed where
it binds: no internal deliberation, board paper, organisation chart or rejected option survives this
window and none was ever required to; **no dated Amazon web page inside the window is used on this
pass**; the FY1999 10-K and its 10-K/A are **2000 documents** and are admissible as *records of
in-window state*, never as what a 1999 reader could know (`ST3E-11`'s status qualifier, applied at every
use); and **every demand quantity in the corpus is the company counting itself**, with no published
definition and no counter-count — customers, accounts, repeat share, visits, Associates enrolments,
registered marketplace users, listings. None of that means the numbers are false. It means they cannot
be checked, and §2's demand-side consequence is that a stage whose growth is measured only by the issuer
must not be narrated as though the market had verified it.

**Confidence (§3):** **High** = 2+ independent sources or a primary document; **Medium** = one reliable

## A. EXECUTIVE STATE SUMMARY

### A.0 The stage in filed terms

At the opening of Stage 3, on 1997-05-16, Amazon was a **Delaware corporation twenty-two months old**,
federally self-classified **SIC 2731**, with **one building doing everything**, a public price of $18.00
one day old, **256 employees** at 1997-03-31 and **$79,000 of working capital** against $16,005k of
quarterly sales `(Amazon S2 §A, part_1)`. What this stage reconstructs is the attempt to convert a
**financed bookseller** into a **network of fulfilment plants plus a recruited seller base**, funded
almost entirely from the equity and debt markets, and never funded from operations inside the window.
**The attempt did not complete by any candidate boundary, and no position claims that it did.**

**FY1997, as filed in FY1997's own report** (`ST3B-02`: the contemporaneous witness governs): net sales
**$147,758k**, gross profit $28,813k, loss from operations **$(29,209)k**, net loss **$(27,590)k**, cash
and equivalents $109,810k, accounts payable **$32,697k**, accumulated deficit $(33,615)k, stockholders'
equity $28,486k, capex $(7,603)k, **614 full-time employees at 1997-12-31**. Re-printed a year later as
comparatives, the same year reads $147,787k / $(32,595)k / **$(31,020)k** — the PlanetAll
pooling-recast (§B.4). **FY1998:** net sales **$609,996k** (**+313%**), net loss **$(124,546)k**,
**+$31,035k** of reported operating cash **but $(41,433)k before changes in operating assets and
liabilities**, accounts payable **$113,273k**, goodwill and intangibles **$186,377k** appearing for the
first time, accumulated deficit $(162,060)k, **~2,100 employees** with the full-time qualifier dropped.
**FY1999**, at the outer working edge and only from a **March-2000** carrier: net sales
**$1,639,839k** (**+169%**), net loss **$(719,968)k**, operating cash flow **$(90,875)k** with the
pre-working-capital subtotal at **$(320,987)k**, capex **$(287,055)k**, accounts payable **$463,026k**,
and **approximately 7,600 full-time and part-time employees** — a **third, wider counting basis**, so
614 → ~2,100 → ~7,600 are **three different measures and not a growth series** (`ST3E-24`).

**The company's own description of what it was doing changed inside the window, and the change is
dated.** The FY1997 report still carried "intends over time to expand its catalog into other
information-based products, such as music" and the Stage-2 objective with its "initial focus on books"
qualifier. The **FY1998 10-K, filed 1999-03-05**, states a new objective with **no category qualifier at
all**: "The Company's objective is to become the best place to buy, find and discover any product or
service available online." (`ST3C-60`.) **This is the single most hindsight-dangerous sentence in the
stage**, and it must be read two ways at once: it is genuinely in-window and genuinely unqualified, and
it is a *statement of objective* filed with no schedule, sequence, resourcing, category revenue line or
capacity plan attached — so it evidences ambition on a date, not capability on a date. Its companion
definition change is the mechanical one: "titles" **dropped the words "a small number of"** between
FY1997 and FY1998, so "2.5 million titles" (1997) and "more than 4.7 million … titles" (1998) are **not
like-for-like counts** and must never be run as a growth ratio (`ST3C-61`, U-C9).

### B.4 The FY1997/FY1996 restatement problem, as an operating fact

### B.7 The operating layer at the recommended boundary: what executed instruments do and do not evidence

date, and its partial defence is recorded here: the Q2-1999 filing is also **the first in which a second
officer signs in person**, and the first in which the founder is described as "**founder and CEO**" with

personnel event — which is why M′ is retained in the corrected test and T′ is not.

unsure of the machine, not of a company that had broken through.**

**Assessment (codas carry the same duty as tables, §16).** The mechanism connecting Stage 3's filings to
Stage 3's outcome is **financed capacity**: capital markets supplied the money, the money bought
buildings, machines and a management layer, and the buildings were still absorbing capital at the edge of
the window (22.7% of gross fixed assets in construction in progress; the largest UK site not yet leased).
What is **not** evidenced is any mechanism by which the operating model itself generated the means to
continue: pre-working-capital cash generation is negative in every year, so on this record **the flywheel
that turns is the financing, not the fulfilment**, and **no independent witness to demand exists at any
date**. Alternative explanation considered and not excluded: that fulfilment cost per sales dollar rose
because of **deliberate pre-building ahead of demand**, which the company itself asserts — "we expect
that fulfillment costs will decline as a percentage of sales in the future as additional capacity of our
existing distribution center network is more fully utilized" — but that sentence is, on the company's own
grammar, **an expectation, not an achieved result**, and no filing in the corpus tests it.

## D. FIRST EXPERIMENT → FIRST REPEATABLE SIGNAL

**Why this section changes shape again.** Stage 1's §D described **one** experiment bounded by a filing
and measured by one audited number; Stage 2 tested **four** repeatable mechanisms, three with no measured
output `(Amazon S2 §D, part_1)`. Stage 3's candidate signals are **structural** and of two kinds —
**capacity built and reported** (plants, machines, estate, rent, capex) and **participation recruited**
(categories, associates, sellers, listings, repeat purchase). Each row keeps the exemplar's geometry and
its most important column is the last: **what the signal did NOT demonstrate**. On the recommended

### D.1 Capacity: the plant problem, in filed lines

The stage's physical record is a **cost series with no output series**, and the asymmetry is the point.
What is filed: **rent** $270,000 → $2.1m → $8.5m (audited — `ST3A-26` calls it "the cleanest single
money-trail of the physical build-out", and it is a **31×** rise 1996→1998); **capex** $(7,603)k →
$(28,333)k → $(287,055)k; **gross fixed assets** $43,585k → **$366,977k** (8.42×) with **$83,290k in
construction in progress** at 1999-12-31; **leased assets** $442k → $52,374k (**118.5×**, DERIVED — the
machine estate is financed, not bought); **depreciation** $3,442k → $9,692k → $36,806k, with **Q4-1999
alone at $13,871k, exceeding all of FY1998** (DERIVED from cumulative 10-Q figures 5,223 / 13,325 /
22,935); and **fulfilment cost** $12.1m → $50.3m → $188.4m, i.e. **8.19% → 8.25% → 11.49% of net sales**.
What is not filed, at any date, in any of the 99 files: **a throughput figure, an order-capacity figure,
a cycle time, an error rate, a fill rate, or a dated outage with a duration**. The nearest approach to a
failure record is two consecutive 10-Qs listing "systems, including temporary power outages at
distribution centers, delayed transportation of" among named failure modes in the controls discussion —
**enumerated, not evidenced as experienced**, and this file does not upgrade them. **Mechanism (§16):**
the spend curve is visible because it is audited; the output curve is absent because it was never
required. **Alternative explanation the record cannot exclude:** that the company could not yet have
produced output numbers, since the plants opened inside the reporting period — which is the same thing as
saying that **at the boundary the network was a construction project being measured by invoices**.

### D.2 Participation: sellers, listings, and the counting problem

Three objects the literature routinely conflates must be held apart: **net sales**, **GMV**, and
**listings**. The corpus contains **only the first**, and from 1999 the first **mixes** own-inventory
price with third-party placement fees, sales commissions and payment-service fees **with no published
split** — so every 1999 growth ratio in this stage is computed on a denominator whose composition changed
mid-run. The single marketplace quantity that exists is "**1 million registered users and 1.5 million
listings**" for Q4-1999, which is: a **count of registrations**, not of sellers who sold; **combined**
across Auctions, zShops and sothebys.amazon.com, so not attributable to any one service; **dated to a
quarter**, not a day; **after both candidate endpoints**, so it cannot rescue A's Q2 transactional leg;
and a **company self-report in its own Item 1**. **`ST3E-13`'s formulation is adopted as the Stage-3
statement of record:** "the only seller-side quantity in the record is a combined registered-user and
listing count for Q4 1999, undisclosed by service and unverified." The same discipline applies to
**"16.9 million cumulative customer accounts"** (basis changed to "inclusive of Auctions" in 1999, so the
1997 and 1998 terms may not be subtracted from it) and to **Associates** (a count of enrolments, yield
unmeasured). **The liability boundary is the more informative filed fact:** the company does not take
responsibility for delivery of payment or goods, relies on sellers' representations, can suspend accounts
but cannot compel performance, and compensates defrauded buyers through a **$250** guarantee it is careful
to call "**not insurance**." **The trust mechanism was therefore launched before its economics were ever
disclosed, and its cost is absent from the record.**

### D.3 Money as signal, and as anti-signal

The stage's genuinely external signals are **financial**, and their honest reading is negative for the
operating model and positive for market belief: a secured lender funding a retailer **against its own
payables** and covenanting their aging (1997-12-23); a public market taking **~$326m** of discount notes
from an issuer that would otherwise have carried **$2.4m** of debt; a convertible market upsizing **$500m
to $1.25bn in six days**; an acquisition-currency shelf tripled twice in fourteen months; a proposed
**1.5-billion-share** authorisation. Each is witnessed outside the registrant and dated, which makes them
the only externally verifiable material in the stage — and none is a statement about unit economics. By
1999 the financing line had become larger than the trading loss it funded: nine-month interest expense
**$(66,424)k** against pre-working-capital operating cash of **$(320,987)k**. **The largest signal in this
window is that the cost of capital fell, not that the cost of doing business did.**

### D.5 Assessment

**What Stage 3's signals, taken together, actually evidence at the recommended boundary.** A
**capacity-and-participation build underwritten by capital markets**, documented at every step by
instruments: nine plants opened across the window, a machine estate contracted before three of its sites
had names, ~$1.58bn of notes and converts raised (DERIVED: 326 + 1,250), an authorised-share ladder multiplied fifteen-fold, a
third-party selling mechanism launched with its liability boundary explicitly drawn, and a management
layer bound by signed offers in the last month of Q3 1999. **What they do not evidence is a model that
reproduced cash.** The pre-working-capital operating subtotal is negative at every year-end in the
window; fulfilment cost per sales dollar **rises**; the first audited quantification of the entire new
business group is a **gross loss**; every demand quantity is self-measured and **two of the series change
definition mid-run**; and the company's own filings in the quarter of C's endpoint predict that the new
plants may not work. **The best-supported reading is therefore narrower than either a triumph or a
failure narrative: by 1999-09-30 this was an organisation that had bought, built and staffed the
apparatus of scale faster than it could have measured it, and had financed that race by selling its own
payables cycle to lenders and its own future to the convertible market.** A **signal was needed on
repeatability in this stage, and none is available on the evidence** — which is itself the stage's
forensic finding, and the reason the §U conflict is about *which axis counts* rather than about a date.
