## BLOCK 6

**>>> SPLICE INTO stage_1.md §U (Conflicting evidence), as new entry U8 — numbering continues across parts
(method §9.3) <<<**

**U8 — the filing's own exhibit recites an impossible corporate form.**
**CLAIM A:** a **Washington** corporation throughout Stage 1, styled "Cadabra, Inc., a Washington corporation"
at subscription 1994-07-05 (Ex. 10.12); Delaware arrived only with Ex. 2.1's merger into "Amazon.com, Inc., a
Delaware corporation," dated 1996-05-28, effective 1996-06-18 [H-29, H-59, H-03].
**CLAIM B:** **Ex. 10.13**, executed "as of this 9th day of February 1995," recites its counterparty as
**"Amazon.com, Inc. … a Delaware corporation"** [H-36] — T1/FACT *that the text reads this way*.
**WHY THEY DIFFER:** B is chronologically impossible as a contemporaneous recital. The 1995 agreement,
assembled into the 1996–97 exhibit set, was evidently conformed to the later corporate form while keeping its
original date (**INFERENCE**, C-H1).
**EVIDENCE WEIGHT:** A — three instruments of one registration statement agree and the Delaware dates are
stated twice; B is a single preamble.
**BEST-SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION:** a **defect in the filing's own text**, not evidence to be smoothed over. In
February 1995 the shareholder contracted with a Washington corporation styled Cadabra, Inc., already trading
under the Amazon.com name; the preamble **cannot** date the rename, the Delaware move, or the 1995 style —
mirror image of COR-02, which confines the Cadabra/1,700,000/sole-stockholder facts to the original.
**RESIDUAL UNCERTAINTY:** whether the style changed by amendment in 1994 or 1995, or only in the 1996 merger,
is unresolvable here; whether Ex. 10.13 replaced an earlier instrument cannot be excluded without the
unreachable Washington registry.
**CONFIDENCE:** High that the recital is internally impossible; **UNKNOWN** as to the style on 1995-02-09.
