# AMAZON STAGE 3 — A. CHRONOLOGY AND ORGANISATION

**Dataset:** THE FOUNDER'S PLAYBOOK — forensic reconstruction, Company 001 Amazon.com (CIK 1018724).
**Stage:** 3 (chronology and organisation). **Opening: 1997-05-16**, the first day on which periodic
filings rather than a registration statement govern. Stage 2's endpoint is closed and inherited here
without re-litigation: IPO effectiveness **1997-05-15**, substantive date **1996-12-31**, Stage-3
opening **1997-05-16** (`stage_2_part_1.md` §STAGE BOUNDARY JUSTIFICATION). **Closing boundary is the
question this dossier answers**, not an assumption: see `## Boundary assessment`.
**Stage definition (method §6):** the earliest defensible point at which the company had become a
**materially scalable operating organisation rather than a promising startup**. IPO, market leadership
and valuation are admissible as endpoints only if one is genuinely the first evidence of that
transition (method §7) — they are not used as endpoints here.
**Hindsight firewall (method §2):** the 1997-99 company did not know it would survive. No decision
below is described as obviously right; no later market outcome is used as evidence about an earlier
choice. Record-selection null: the surviving archive is the **winner's** filing record, so it is dense
on capital markets and thin on operations. Internal organisation charts, rejected options, warehouse
opening events never filed, and any independent headcount verification are **unrecoverable because
they were never public** — this dossier's operational spine is therefore partly a *disclosure* spine
(§6 of method §14 rule 6: four families, one of which — periodicals — is untried here and is recorded
as UNTRIED, not as a null).
**Confidence scale:** High (2+ independent sources or primary document) · Medium (one reliable source)
· Low (conflicting/retrospective-only) · UNKNOWN.

**Filing-lineage rule (method §3), applied throughout.** Registration File No. 333-23795 (S-1
original + Amendments Nos. 1-6 + 424B1 + the 8-A12G that incorporates it) is **one source**. Each S-4,
S-3, S-8 and its own amendments/POS AM/424 supplements is **one lineage**. A 10-K and its exhibits are
one filing. Repeated copying across a lineage never counts as corroboration; corroboration below is
only from *outside* the lineage (a different form family, an auditor's separate report, an
acquired-company's own statements, a third-party filing).

**Two traps carried from the intake, both binding here.**
1. `8-A12G` (acc. 0000891020-97-000704, filed 1997-05-02) Item 1 misdates the prospectus as "dated

   is never cited for a 1996 date.
2. **Constant 17-line offset** between the two local copies of the same accession. Line numbers in

   part of the citation. The four S-1/A files exist twice (hyphen and underscore forms, byte-identical
   bodies); **the hyphenated names are cited** per `STAGE3_INTAKE_MANIFEST.md` §8.

**Post-boundary discipline runs forward from here:** everything after this dossier's chosen endpoint
is tagged `(PB)` — post-boundary — and is not folded into the window it describes. Stage-2 material is
