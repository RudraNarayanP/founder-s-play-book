### THE FOUNDER'S PLAYBOOK — Amazon.com, Inc. — Stage 3 — Part C: Product, Category, Supply, Distribution, Competition, Market

**Dataset:** THE FOUNDER'S PLAYBOOK — forensic reconstruction of Fortune-50 companies' early histories from public evidence only.
**Company:** Amazon.com, Inc. (CIK 0001018724; SIC 2731 at the Stage-2 boundary).
**Stage:** 3. **Window opened: 1997-05-16** (fixed by Stage 2's boundary; not re-litigated here). Working close for this part: 1999-12-31, with every 1999 record carrying its document date.
**Stage definition (as inherited):** the period in which the Stage-2 question — *does the model repeat* — is put to the company by the company itself, through category expansion, acquisitions, multi-site fulfilment and the first third-party selling arrangements.
**Hindsight-firewall statement:** nothing in this file is graded on outcomes after 1999. Where a later fate is known to this project (Amazon's eventual category dominance, the eventual success or failure of WarehouseDirect, PlanetAll, Accept.com, zShop, Amazon Auctions, the UK/German stores' profitability, the 2000 dot-com collapse), it is **excluded, not down-weighted**. The 1998–99 filings already contain the company's own victory claims ("number one online music seller"); they are recorded as *company self-measurement with no denominator*, never as market fact, and they are not read forward.
**Confidence scale:** High (2+ independent sources or a primary document) · Medium (one reliable source, or approximate date corroborated later) · Low (conflicting, vague, retrospective-only) · UNKNOWN (no evidence recovered). **Filing-lineage rule (§3) applied throughout:** all 1997–99 Amazon documents below are the **same registrant lineage**; corroboration inside one accession, or between an accession and its own prospectus/ARS reprint, is **one source**. Independence is claimed only against (i) another registrant's filing (Barnes & Noble, Book Stacks/CUC, Ingram-adjacent third parties), (ii) an auditor, (iii) contemporaneous press not derived from the filing, or (iv) a counterparty's signature block.
**Post-boundary tag:** `(PB)` marks a figure whose basis is outside a given section's stated date; used where this part quotes Stage 2 forward.

**Three strongest channel findings, stated with their limits.** (1) **Associates**: an input counted twice in one year (140,000 → ~200,000) and still with no cost and no yield anywhere — Stage 2's "claimed and never measured" verdict is not cured, and the growth of the count is therefore not evidence of anything except enrolment. (2) **Acquired local sites**: the only distribution move in the window with an instrument (the April 1998 purchases), a relaunch date (October 1998) and a filed result (Q4-1998 "nearly quadrupled"; 25% of Q4 sales from the new businesses together).
