**Company/stage:** Amazon.com, Inc., 1996 through the first hard evidence of a repeatable model. The May 1997 IPO is tested as a candidate boundary, not assumed (see S2C-01/02 and "Boundary verdict" in Narrative notes).
**Hindsight firewall:** every competitor is described only from documents dated on or before the boundary evidence used here (FY1997 annual report, filed 1998-03-30, is the last Stage-2 document admitted). Nothing is graded by outcomes after 1997. Where a post-1997 document is used, it is used **only** to date an in-window event and is labelled as such.
**Reuse policy:** Stage-1 established market facts are cited from `C_market_environment.md` (records C-nn) and `D_customers_distribution.md` (records D-nn) rather than re-derived. Documented nulls inherited: no archived amazon.com page for 1995-1996-1997 (D-21, `NULL_RESULT_wayback_1995_1996.md`); no contemporaneous founder-state interview exists — the Sheff material is Playboy, conducted 1999 / published 2000 (`K_roadmap_provenance.md` K-01, K-27, and `_EVIDENCE_CACHE.md` C-1). **Every strategy statement in this dossier is therefore sourced to a filing, a dated press release or a dated press article, and no claim here rests on founder recollection.**
