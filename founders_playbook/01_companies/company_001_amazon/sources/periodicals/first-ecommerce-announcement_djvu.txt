Announcement of Historical E-Commerce Milestone 


It is hereby declared that the very first online e-commerce shopping website was envisioned and 
intended to be launched at the domain: www.InternationalShoppingGroupLtd.com This 
groundbreaking initiative was the creation of Larry Hamilton and Lloyd Hamilton, who set out to 
pioneer online shopping as we know it today. Their vision was to establish a platform where consumers 
could access a wide range of products through a convenient digital marketplace, marking the beginning 
of a new era in global commerce. This announcement serves as an official recognition of their 
foundational role in shaping the future of e-commerce. 


Issued in recognition of the contributions of Larry & Lloyd Hamilton
