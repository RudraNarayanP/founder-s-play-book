FOREWORD 


The exhibition resulting from this year's competition contains, besides the work 
of established painters, a considerable number of paintings by younger artists 
whose ability and seriousness of purpose should result in the further strengthening 
of American Art. 

Pepsi-Cola Company is happy to give recognition and encouragement to these 
youthful artists through its annual art project as well as serve those painters who 
are also well known. 

This year, in addition to the twenty cash awards totaling $15,250. and the 
continuance of the fellowships in painting of $1500. each, one to each region, the 
Company has purchased outright from the exhibition eleven paintings valued at 
$14,700. for reproduction in full color on its 1948 calendar. 

The Company is also pleased to announce the first presentation in this ex¬ 
hibition of the Pepsi-Cola Achievement Award Medal, designed by the noted 
sculptor, William Zoracli, to ten artists whose work was adjudged the next best 
in the exhibition by the Jury of Awards. 

The substantial nature of the awards and purchases made by the Company this 
year and the distribution throughout the country of approximately 650,000 calen¬ 
dars containing reproductions in color of selected paintings from the exhibition 
will we trust add further recognition to the growing interest in American art. 

Walter S. Mack, Jr., President 
PEPSI-COLA COMPANY 

Roland McKinney,. Director
