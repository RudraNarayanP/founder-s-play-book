OTH ANNUAL CENTENNIAL BASKETBALL TOURNAMENT SET. 
SPONSORED BY PEPSI COLA CANADA LTD. 


It has been announced that the 9th Annual Centennial Basketball 
Tournament will be played at Ioyola's Athletic Complex on Friday and 
Saturday, December 6th and 7th. Friday's games are slated for 7 and 9 p.m. 
and on Saturday for 6:30 and 8:30 p.m. 


This year's tourney will be sponsored by Pepsi Cola Canada Ltd. 
as part of their expanding contribution to university athletics in Canada. 


The event was first established in 1967 by Loyola's Director of 
Physical Education and Athletics, Dr. Ed mos, and has grown to the point 
where it rates as one of the top tournaments in the country. Each year the 
caliber of play has improved and this year will be no exception. 


The four teams selected to play in this year's tournament are 
the defending champions, Acadia University from Wolfville, Nova Scotia, 
St. Francis Xavier University from Antigonish, Nova Scotia, Mec Master 
University from Hamilton, Ontario, as well as the host Loyola Warriors. 


This is the first year that the tourney has featured four 
Canadian squads, the format in the past having included two U.S. entries. 
The reason for the change is that the Tournament Selection Committee felt 
that Canadian College basketball has developed to the point where good 
Canadian teams compare favorably with the U.S. squads invited in the past. 


The Axemen from Acadia became the first Canadian entry to walk 
off with top honors when they defeated a tough R.P.I. squad in overtime 
to win last year's event. The Axemen then went on to win the strong 
Maritime conference title and are once again regarded as one of the 
premier teams in the nation. Leading the way for Coach Richard Hunt 
will be two outstanding big men, 6'5" All-Canadian Joey Wells and 6ryn 
rookie Gene Saunders.

The St. Francis Xavier X-Men have consistently fielded contending teams

Coach Bill Fowler's Mc Master Marauders are coming off a third place 
finish in Ontario's highly regarded West Division and boast two of the league's

The host Loyola Warriors will be relying on the front line of 6'5"
