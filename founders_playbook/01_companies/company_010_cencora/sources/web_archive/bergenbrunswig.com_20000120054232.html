ROBERT E. MARTINI

Robert E. Martini is chairman of the board of Bergen Brunswig Corporation, a post to which he brings more than 40 years of experience in the pharmaceutical industry.

Born in Hackensack, New Jersey, Mr. Martini was educated at Ohio State University College of Pharmacy, where he received a bachelor of science degree in 1954.

Mr. Martini joined Bergen Drug Company, the predecessor to Bergen Brunswig, in 1956, after serving as an officer in the U.S. Air Force. He held various management positions before his election to president in 1981, chief executive officer in 1990 and chairman of the board in 1992.

Mr. Martini serves on a number of professional advisory boards and community organizations.
