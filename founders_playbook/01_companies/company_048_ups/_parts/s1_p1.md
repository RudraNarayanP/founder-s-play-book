### Hindsight firewall

Nothing here is written backward from UPS's later scale. The 1999 numbers in §P and §T are used only as
evidence of what the registrant said about itself in 1999, never as proof that 1907-1927 choices were
correct, obvious or inevitable. Anti-hagiography test applied to every section: if UPS had shut down in
1935, would this part still read the same? — it would, because it claims almost nothing about the period.
**Record-selection null (§2), stated for the period:** UPS was private and employee-owned with no filing duty
until 1999, so the archive that survived is a self-narrative plus its registration; internal deliberations,
rejected options, failures and any independent count of 1907-1960 parcels, capital or wages are unrecoverable
here for a structural reason, not a retrieval failure. The company's own S-1 prints the consequence: its
common stock "is not publicly traded" (S-1 l.274).
