## 5. What I refused to claim — WRITTEN

1. **Refused: a 1900-1908 Buick or Durant hit as a naming of the registrant.** Nothing held names the 1908 *General Motors Company* inside a 1900-1908 document; the earliest *held* naming is 1920-01-01 (`daytonwrightairp00gene` L31), and the 1908 date reaches us only through a **1928 retrospective** (L9462–9470) and a **1915 annual report quoted second-hand** (L10666–10667 + L10678). Per the J&J precedent in RD-130, a retrospective founding statement is a **range of testimony**, not the date itself. **The naming wall for GM is therefore 1920 for a document that names, and 1908 for a date asserted — and those are different claims.**
2. **Refused the reverse error: dating the company to its acquired assets.** `daytonwrightairp00gene` **L172** "In 1908, we developed a power machine" is **Orville Wright's first person** (L24 "THE BIRTHPLACE OF AVIATION", L34 "Orville Wright"), printed in 1920 under a copyright held by *The General Motors Corporation, Dayton-Wright Division* (L31).
