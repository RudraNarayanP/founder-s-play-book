 <P STYLE="margin-top:0px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2"><B>Item&nbsp;5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain
Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers </B></FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:6px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2"><B><U>New Equity Awards</U></B> </FONT></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:6px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">On June&nbsp;30, 2011, the spin-off of Marathon Petroleum Corporation (&#147;MPC,&#148; &#147;we&#148; or &#147;us&#148;) from Marathon Oil Corporation
(&#147;MRO&#148;) was completed through the distribution of shares of our common stock to holders of outstanding shares of MRO&#146;s common stock (the &#147;Spin-off&#148;). In connection with the Spin-off, we entered into an Employee Matters
Agreement with MRO. We filed a copy of the Employee Matters Agreement with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the &#147;SEC&#148;) on May&nbsp;26, 2011 as an exhibit to a Registration Statement on Form 10 (the &#147;Form 10&#148;). The Employee
Matters Agreement was amended on June&nbsp;30, 2011 (as amended, the &#147;EMA&#148;), and we filed a copy of the Amendment to Employee Matters Agreement with the SEC on July&nbsp;1, 2011 as an exhibit to a Current Report on Form 8-K. </FONT></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">At the time of the Spin-off, outstanding equity awards that had been previously granted pursuant to the MRO Stock Plans (as such term is defined within
the EMA) to our directors and employees were adjusted and new MPC equity awards were granted pursuant to the terms of the EMA (the &#147;Spin-off Adjusted Equity Awards&#148;). As noted in the Form 10, the Spin-off Adjusted Equity Awards were
intended to generally preserve the intrinsic value of the original MRO awards. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">The methodology set forth within the EMA to calculate the
Spin-off Adjusted Equity Awards relied on a ratio of the simple average of the high and low trading prices reported for a share of MRO common stock for June&nbsp;30, 2011 and the simple averages of the high and low trading prices reported for a
share of MRO and MPC common stock for July&nbsp;1, 2011. However, on July&nbsp;1, during a period of heightened volatility of approximately 30 seconds in duration, trades of MPC common stock occurred on other exchanges at prices well above the
day&#146;s trading range on the NYSE primary market. These trades had a significant adverse effect on the calculation of the Spin-off Adjusted Equity Awards. Consequently, on December&nbsp;5, 2011, our Board approved new grants of approximately
13,000 MPC restricted stock units, approximately 9,700 shares of MPC restricted stock and options for approximately 363,000 shares of MPC common stock to a total of approximately 300 recipients, including six of the non-employee members of our Board
and the five named executive officers set forth below. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">The number of new MPC restricted stock units, shares of MPC restricted stock and MPC
stock options granted to each recipient was determined through an assessment by our Board in consultation with its independent compensation consultant of the adverse effect of the aberrant trades on the Spin-off Adjusted Equity Awards. The exercise
price for all of the new options is the closing stock price of MPC common stock on the grant date of December&nbsp;5, 2011. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">All of these new
grants were made pursuant to the Marathon Petroleum Corporation Second Amended and Restated 2011 Incentive Compensation Plan (the &#147;Plan&#148;). </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT
STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">The new MPC equity awards described in this Current Report on Form 8-K include the following awards granted to the MPC named executive officers listed below: </FONT></P>
