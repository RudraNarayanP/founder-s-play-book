<P STYLE="color: #2292D0; font: 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0pt 12.4pt 0pt 9pt">Determination of Interest Payments</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0pt 12.4pt 0pt 9pt">On each interest payment date, the amount of each interest
payment will equal (i) the stated principal amount of the notes <I>multiplied by</I> the interest rate, <I>multiplied by </I>(ii) (180/360).

in the payment you receive upon redemption will equal (i) the stated principal amount of the notes <I>multiplied by</I> the interest rate,
<I>multiplied by </I>(ii) (90/360).</P>
