<P align="left"><FONT size="2"><B>Item&nbsp;5. OTHER EVENTS.</B></FONT>

<P><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On September&nbsp;18, 2001, the Company issued the following press release:
</FONT>
<P align="center"><FONT size="2"><B>Boeing Responds to Current Business Environment</B></FONT>

<P>
<TABLE width="100%" border="0" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0">
<TR valign="top">
   <TD width="1%" nowrap><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="1%" align="left"><FONT size="2">&#149;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="1%" nowrap><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="97%"><FONT size="2">Taking rapid action to respond to the current airline
industry crisis</FONT></TD>
</TR>
<TR>
        <TD><FONT size="2">&nbsp;</FONT></TD>
</TR>
<TR valign="top">
   <TD width="1%" nowrap><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="1%" align="left"><FONT size="2">&#149;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="1%" nowrap><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="97%"><FONT size="2">Working with the U.S. government to develop safe and secure
air transport system</FONT></TD>
</TR>
<TR>
        <TD><FONT size="2">&nbsp;</FONT></TD>
</TR>
<TR valign="top">
   <TD width="1%" nowrap><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="1%" align="left"><FONT size="2">&#149;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="1%" nowrap><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;</FONT></TD>
   <TD width="97%"><FONT size="2">Leveraging strong technological capabilities in defense and
space to support the U.S. government</FONT></TD>
</TR>
</TABLE>
<P><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Boeing is taking a series of actions to address the changed business
environment in light of last week&#146;s terrorist attacks against the United
States.
</FONT>
<P><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Given the impacts of the horrific attacks of September&nbsp;11, and the
resulting capacity reductions now anticipated by its airline customers,
Boeing&#146;s Commercial Airplanes unit is setting in place plans to reduce its
employment by approximately 20,000 to 30,000 people by the end of 2002. This
includes a reduction in force in related support personnel at Boeing&#146;s Shared
Services Group.
</FONT>
<P><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&#147;We profoundly regret that these actions will impact the lives of so many
of our highly-valued employees,&#148; said Alan Mulally, president and CEO of Boeing
Commercial Airplanes. &#147;However, it is critical that we take these necessary
steps now to size the Commercial Airplanes business to support the difficult
and uncertain environment faced by our airline customers.&#148;
</FONT>
<P><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Revised delivery projections for Boeing Commercial Airplanes, which are
assessments based on an uncertain future and depend upon customer business
decisions, are as follows: Deliveries for 2001, which had been expected to be
538 aircraft, could be as low as 500 depending on customer ability to take
delivery in the near term. For 2002, deliveries are estimated to be in the low
400s, compared to the 510 to 520 previously forecast. Current estimates for
2003 indicate a downward delivery trend will continue. Boeing will add further
clarity about 2003 as it gains insight into the market&#146;s direction. These
estimates are in line with the initial 20&nbsp;percent capacity reduction
projections provided by U.S. carriers and its assessment of world airline
traffic trends.
</FONT>
<P><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The company is working closely with the government on improving air
transport safety and security. It has also launched an internal initiative
under the direction of its chief technology officer to solicit and review ideas
from Boeing engineers for improving these areas. At the same time, the company
is working to apply its extensive capabilities in intelligence gathering,
communications and space, as well as military aircraft and missiles, in the war
against terrorism.
</FONT>
<P align="center"><FONT size="2">&nbsp;
</FONT>

<P><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&#147;The company is ready to apply all of its wide-ranging technological
resources in stepping up to the challenges created by these tragic events,&#148;
said Boeing chairman and CEO Phil Condit. &#147;We are making tough business
decisions and working closely with our commercial and government customers to
rebuild public confidence in a safe and secure U.S. air transport system.&#148;
</FONT>
<P><FONT size="2">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&#147;These actions will also enhance the company&#146;s ability to maintain its
solid financial position, strong liquidity and premier debt ratings. These are
critical factors in times of business uncertainty and financial stress,&#148; Condit
said.
</FONT>
