Valero Refinery Aruba awards a record amount in donations to Aruban foundation 
On Thursday afternoon management and staff of the Valero Refinery Aruba representing the 


Valero Energy Corporation had the enjoyable task of presenting checks Aruban Foundations devoted to 
assisting children and the mentally and physically challenged. The individual amounts ranged from 5,000 
to 25,000 Aruban florins for a total of 377,360. Aruban florins ($215,634. U.S.) donated to worthy island 
charities. 
The funds are a portion of the proceeds from the record-breaking seven million dollars raised at 


the Valero Texas Open and the Valero Benefit for Children Gold Classic in San Antonio, Texas in 
September of 2006. The Valero Texas Open is an official PGA tour event played at the La Cantera course 
that attracted 144 top PGA professionals, and is the third oldest professional golf tournament in the United 
States. 
The Valero Children’s Golf Classic was established in 1996, and has raised more than $17.5 


million dollars for children’s charities around the world, in communities wherever Valero has business 
operations. This past tournament set a record with 2,000 participants playing on eight gold courses and is 
conducted on the heels of the Valero Texas Open. An unprecedented seventeen companies sponsored the 
event, pledging amounts between $100,000 and $500,000 each for children’s charities. Ninety percent of 
the funds raised by the two tournaments come from this event. 
The seven million dollars raised is a new record in PGA tour history, and the Valero Texas Open 


set a record for the fifth consecutive year as the top charitable fundraiser on the PGA Tour. 
More than 500 nonprofit organizations worldwide will benefit from the funds raised by the two 


tournaments. At the Westin Resort in Palm Beach, Raymond Buckley, vice-president and general manager 
of Valero Refinery Aruba and members of the refinery’s workforce presented the donations to 
representatives of the twenty-five Aruban charities during a special ceremony. Seth Martijn, boat captain 
for Valero repeated his duties as Master of Ceremonies. 
The community agencies receiving donations perform a variety of services for children and are 


dedicated to improving their lives. These services range from providing shelter and counseling for abused 
and abandoned children to educational and recreational programs for youth. Sports organizations that 
promote healthy pastimes for youth and the handicapped, Scouting, and the YMCA are an example of the 
many programs that were recognized as assisting island children.

The organizations were nominated and selected by the employees of the Valero Refinery Aruba, 
and this is the third consecutive year that the refinery has made such awards in Aruba, with the 
number of organizations and amounts growing larger each year. Charities in the U.S. and Canada 
have also benefited from the proceeds of the tournaments, which won the Valero Corporation and 
the Valero Texas Open the Benefactor Award from the PGA Tour Tournaments Association. 
(PGATTA) Valero was also recognized by the PGATTA for surpassing twenty million dollars in 
charitable contributions. Prior to Valero assuming sponsorship in 2002, the tournament event had 
raised 4.6 million dollars for charity. Since becoming the Valero Texas Open, twenty-one million 
dollars has been raised, setting the record at $25.6 million. In a final address to the gathering, Ray 
Buckley stated how proud Valero was to be able to assist the worthwhile work of the 
organizations, and encouraged the gathering to offer a round of applause for the foundation 
members that work tirelessly to provide for, protect, and guide Aruba’s youth.
