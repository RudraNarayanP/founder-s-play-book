ITEM 5. OTHER EVENTS.

         On April 10, 2002, Valero Energy Corporation (the "Company") entered
into an underwriting agreement for the public offering of $300,000,000 aggregate
principal amount of its 6 1/8% Notes due 2007, $750,000,000 aggregate principal
amount of its 6 7/8% Notes due 2012 and $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount
of its 7 1/2% Notes due 2032 (the "Notes"). The Notes are to be issued under an
Indenture dated December 12, 1997 between the Company and The Bank of New York,
as Trustee. The Notes were registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as
amended, pursuant to the shelf registration statement (Registration Nos.
333-84820, 333-84820-1 and 333-84820-2) of the Company, VEC Trust III and VEC
Trust IV. Closing of the issuance and sale of the Notes is scheduled for April
15, 2002.
