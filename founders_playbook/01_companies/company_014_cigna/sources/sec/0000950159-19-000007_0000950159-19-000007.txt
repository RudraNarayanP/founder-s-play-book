        <div style="text-align: left; margin-bottom: 8pt; font-family: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif; font-size: 10pt;"><font style="background-color: #FFFFFF;">Cigna Corporation (&#8220;Cigna&#8221; or the &#8220;Company&#8221;) officials expect to participate in meetings with
            investors and analysts on January 7 and 8, 2019 at the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference, including a presentation by David M. Cordani, Cigna's President and Chief Executive Officer. During these meetings, Cigna officials expect to disclose
            expectations for growth in 2019, including global medical customer growth of 300,000 to 400,000 customers over Cigna&#8217;s year-end 2018 global medical customer base, including mid-single digit&#160;percentage&#160;growth in individual Medicare Advantage
            customers over year-end 2018, and continued strong growth in international markets. In addition, Cigna management also intends to affirm Express Scripts Holding Company&#8217;s previously reported expectation for growth of 2% to 3% in core business
            adjusted claims in 2019.</font></div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div style="text-align: left;"><font style="font-size: 10pt; font-family: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif;">In addition, in order to align with the planned integration of Express Scripts Holding Company, Cigna and OptumRx have entered into a mutual
        agreement for the transition of the pharmacy benefit management services currently provided by OptumRx. The individual services, which include non-specialty drug mail order fulfillment, Medicare claims processing, and retail contracting, are
        expected to transition at different dates, with all services expected to be transitioned and completed by December 31, 2020. &#160;</font></div>
    <div><br>
    </div>
    <div style="text-align: left;"><font style="font-size: 10pt; font-family: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif;">Cigna&#8217;s full year 2018 results will be discussed in a press release and investor conference call scheduled for February 1, 2019.</font></div>
    <div><br>
    </div>
    <div style="text-align: left;"><font style="font-size: 10pt; font-family: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif;">Cigna's presentation at the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference is expected to begin at approximately 4:00 p.m. PST on January 7, 2019.&#160;
        Investors, analysts and the general public are invited to listen to the presentation free over the Internet via webcast by visiting http://www.cigna.com and clicking on About Cigna, then Investor Relations, then the Investor Events link.&#160; To listen
        to this presentation live on the Internet, visit http://www.cigna.com at least 15 minutes prior to the presentation to download and install any necessary audio software.</font></div>
    <div><br>

    <div style="text-align: left;"><font style="font-size: 10pt; font-family: 'Times New Roman', Times, serif;">&#8220;Global medical customers&#8221; include individuals who meet any one of the following criteria:&#160; are covered under a medical insurance policy,
        managed care arrangement, or service agreement issued by Cigna; have access to Cigna's provider network for covered services under their medical plan; or have medical claims and services that are administered by Cigna.&#160; &#8220;Adjusted claims&#8221; includes
        an adjustment to reflect non-specialty network claims filled through 90-day programs. These claims are multiplied by three, as these claims, on average, typically cover a time period three times longer than other network claims. Home delivery
        claims are also multiplied by three as home delivery claims typically cover a time period three times longer than unadjusted network claims. All other network and specialty claims are counted as one claim.&#160; &#8220;Core business&#8221; excludes the
