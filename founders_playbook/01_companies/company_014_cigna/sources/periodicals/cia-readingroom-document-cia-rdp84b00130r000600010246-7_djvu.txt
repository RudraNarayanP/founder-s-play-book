9 April 1980 


MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD 


Staff Meeting Minutes of 9 April 1980 


Mr. Carlucci chaired the meeting. 


Lehman said he is considering preparation of an Alert Memorandum on 
developing hostilities between Iran and Iraq. In response to a query from 
Mr. Carlucci, Lehman said we are not sure how the situation will develop 
militarily, noting however that Iraq can be expected to conduct subversion. 
He noted also Iraq's military divisions have not been deployed in response 
to Iran's most recent threat. 


Fitzwater reported clericals in process, which hopefully will 
alleviate current shortages in this job category. He reported also 
Connecticut General has agreed to a withdrawal of 50 percent 
from the Agency's VIP income fund for investment via the Credit Union in 
higher interest Treasury certificates--14 percent versus 8 percent. He 
opined that Connecticut General agreed in order to save the VIP account; he 
noted the is to be returned to the income fund at the end of 6 


months. 


Lipton reported work is well underway with the Agency managers in 
developing the 1982 budget. He said also his office is preparing for next 
week's Congressional markup of the 1981 budget. A brief discussion followed 
on how our 1981 budget amendment request to OMB will fare. Hitz expressed 
his concern for how the amendment will be handled on the Hill and what our 
recourse would be in the event of denial or serious shortfall. 
opined that OMB will be supportive and, despite a potential for problems on 


the Hill, the amendment is likely to survive. 


Hetu called attention to hearings to be held by Representative Aspin 
next week re prepublication review procedures. Hitz noted DIA, NSA, and 
FBI representatives are scheduled to testify on 16 April and that, in a 
letter to the Director, HPSCI Chairman Boland said he would "appreciate" an 
Agency representative in attendance. Noting Hetu had already testified, 
it was agreed that Hetu accompanied by should appear again on the 
16th. 


Hitz called attention to Representative Aspin's intent to schedule 
another hearing on 18 April re the Presidential Finding relating to 
Noting that Mr. Carlucci had testified in detail on 1 April, 
Hitz said Representative Aspin was not in attendance. Mr. Carlucci agreed 
with Hitz's suggestion that we seek deferral of Mr. Carlucci's reappearance 
and that we would provide responses to questions for the record.

Hitz called Mr. Carlucci's attention to a special reprogramming 
action which has apparently disturbed Senator Mathias. He said Senator 
Mathias has complained to Senator Baker re committee handling/coordination 
of the action. A brief discussion followed re Hill staffers’ culpability. 
(Later in the meeting, Mr. Carlucci asked Hitz to find out if a phone call 


to Senator Mathias might help to alleviate the Senator's concern.) 


Hitz said he would appreciate some time from each Deputy Director to 
meet with Peter Murphy, HAC Staffer (Snodgrass' successor), who is visiting 
Headquarters for most of the day. Based on their recent meetings with 
Murphy, | gave some brief insights on how best to deal 
with him; noted Murphy is "quick" in his ability to grasp; 
noted Murphy's interest in Cuba and North Korea. Hitz briefly summarized 
Murphy's extensive experience in government and said Murphy can be expected 
to be "tough" in his HAC role. 


Hitz said OLC is conducting a "legislative congress" this 
weekend, and that the session will include attendees from CTS, RMS, and 
other Community organizations. Discussion will focus on such items as 
review of Charters Legislation and the budget process. 


Silver described the SSCI staff preparation of a mini-charter as an 
"abomination." He cited numerous weaknesses in the product and said the 
product is likely to end the charter process for this year. He noted also 
that this mini-charter is generally more restrictive than the fuller (170 
page) version. 


Ward said Dirks was visiting NSA today and Taylor was at the National 
Bureau of Standards.
