automotive grade lithium-ion battery cells will be used in Tesla&#146;s premium electric sedan, Model S. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">The agreement supplies Tesla with

existing Model S reservations.&nbsp;This supply agreement helps ensure Tesla will meet its cost and margin targets for Model S. </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">This
agreement builds upon a multi-year collaboration between Panasonic and Tesla to develop next-generation automotive-grade battery cells and accelerate the market expansion of electric vehicles. In 2009, Panasonic and Tesla initially entered into a

cell based on this nickel chemistry and optimized specifically for electric vehicle quality and life.&nbsp;These new cells will combine with Tesla&#146;s proven EV battery expertise gained from more than 15&nbsp;million customer miles driven in
Tesla Roadsters and thousands of hours of cell and battery testing to create the most capable electric vehicle ever produced, Model S. </FONT></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">&#147;It is a powerful endorsement of our technology that Panasonic, the world&#146;s leading battery cell manufacturer, has chosen to partner with Tesla
to advance electric vehicle performance and value,&#148; said Tesla Co-Founder and CEO Elon Musk. &#147;Incorporating Panasonic&#146;s next-generation cells into Model S batteries will ensure unrivaled range and performance. We are very grateful for
our strong partnership with Panasonic.&#148; </FONT></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0px"><FONT STYLE="font-family:Times New Roman" SIZE="2">&#147;Panasonic will supply lithium-ion cells for EVs that can achieve longer range with large
