HUANG-TIEN-FAN STATE FARM IN CHEKIANG 


A_state operated mechanized farm project covering an area of 39,000 
mou [one mou equals 1/6 acre] at Huang-tien-fan on the north bank of 
Fu-ch'un Chiang is being completed by the Chekiang Provincial govern- 
ment. It is to be used as a training center and pilot farm for future 
mechanized farms in the province. The land has been made vroductive 
through reclamation and flood contro] works which were started Sep- 
tember 1950 and have employed over 10,095 workers. 


Hangchow -- To acquire experience in tne field of mechanized farming and to 
introduce progressive farming methods to the farmers, the Agriculture and Foreatry 
Department of Chekiang, in September 1950, started the construction of a state 
farm at Huang-tien-fan in Fu-yang Hsien. At present, the farm project is being 
coupleted with enthusiastic assistance from the local government and people. 


The farm, covering 39,000 mou is located on the north bank of Fu-ch'un 
Chiang. The river front of the farm fields is about 6 141 /one 11 equais 1/3 mile/
