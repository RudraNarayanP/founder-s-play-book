## 3. Teleology in boundaries and framing — **FAIL at 2 sites (same defect)**

The argument that the IPO is where substance "became externally verifiable" is stated as an argument and defended:
`part_1:28` ends "where assertions became **externally verifiable**, not where the business was proven"; `:57` "What
the boundary does NOT claim" (i)–(v) bars certification of repeatability, first-trade, stability, price comparability
and later apparatus; the substantive boundary (1996-12-31) is *carried*, not discarded, and named as where
"repeatability actually shows"; U.102 leaves the boundary disagreement un-merged and records that B/C "rest on
`(PB)` facts this part may cite but not use to re-draw the stage". That is the correct treatment of a contestable
periodisation.

**But the justification list over-reaches in the direction of making the IPO the moment the company became real.**

- `stage_2_part_1.md:28` — "**where the firm first held its Delaware form, officers, board, auditors' consent and a
  public price**". By the file's own §B.0 (`:91–93`), §Q (`part_3:49–53`) and U.78: the Delaware form existed from
  **1996-06-18**, the board and the office of CEO were created on the **1996-05-28** merger instrument, Covey was CFO
  from **1996-12**, and the Series A paper was executed **1996-06-21**. The company held all three of those
  eleven months before the boundary — and §B.0 (`:93`) still records a **CIO seat unfilled** and a **CTO title four
  weeks old** at pricing, so "officers … first held" is false in both directions.
- `stage_2_part_1.md:52` — the "Why the IPO is the cut" row: "Delaware form (effected 1996-06-18 …), officers, board,
  signed financing paper (Series A 1996-06-21), auditors' consent, a public price, reporting duties — **all in place
  only from 05-14/15**". The sentence contradicts the dates printed inside it, and `:41` justifies the same endpoint
  by listing the 1996 reincorporation and the 1996 Series A closing — items that belong to Stage 2's *contents*, not
  to its *end*.

**Why this matters and not just cosmetically:** three of the six nouns are 1996 items, so the enumeration recruits
the whole institutional history of the stage into a claim whose only real carriers are the auditors' consent, the
price, effectiveness and continuous reporting. Narrowed to those four the boundary stands (U.102's "A is the only
position with a dated legal instrument on the boundary date"); padded with the other three it reads as the moment
the business became an institution, which is the teleology §2 bars. **C-9**: delete "its Delaware form, officers,
board" from `:28` and from `:52`; at `:41` re-label the 1996 items as *stage-opening* evidence and keep 05-14/15
resting on consent + price + effectiveness + reporting duties. **C-10**: `stage_2_part_3.md:22` still cross-refers to
"part_1 **l.18**" for the three-date cluster after the AUDIT-2 DEFECT-9 repair re-pointed part_1's own self-pointer to
l.21/l.15 — the same stale-pointer class as Stage 1's F-5.
